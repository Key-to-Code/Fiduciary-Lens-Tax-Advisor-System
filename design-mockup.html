Under §80C of the Income Tax Act, you can claim deductions up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year against gross total income. Eligible avenues include life-insurance premiums, EPF & PPF contributions, ELSS mutual funds, tuition fees for up to two children, principal repayment on a home loan, and certain fixed deposits.

The limit is a combined ceiling across all these instruments — it is not ₹1.5L per item. Deduction is available only under the old tax regime; the new regime §115BAC does not allow §80C.
